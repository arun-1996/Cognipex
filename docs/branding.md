# Branding

## Status

The working name is **Cogpace**. The theme now has a working visual identity, but
the name and mark still require legal and trademark review before they are treated
as a finalized brand specification.

## Working identity

- **Primary logo:** `wp-content/themes/cogpace/assets/images/cogpace-logo.png`
  is a 1024 × 244 transparent PNG containing the brain and Cogpace wordmark.
- **Mark:** `wp-content/themes/cogpace/assets/images/cogpace-mark.png` is a
  512 × 512 transparent PNG for compact and favicon-scale uses.
- **Concept:** a simplified brain outline and three connected nodes represent
  cognition, focused learning, practice, and connected ideas without resembling a
  medical symbol or detailed anatomical illustration.
- **Colors:** brand blue (`#93C5FD`) and accent teal (`#5EEAD4`) on transparent.
- **Wordmark:** `Cogpace` is embedded in the primary visual lockup. The configured
  WordPress Site Title remains in the header link as its accessible name.

The header image is decorative because the Site Title provides the link's
accessible name. When either asset is used as content elsewhere, give it the
alternative text `Cogpace`. Keep the compact mark at least 24 CSS pixels wide and
the primary lockup at least 150 CSS pixels wide on screen. Do not distort, recolor,
rotate, shadow, or place either asset on a low-contrast background.

## Pending approvals

- Legal name, trademark status, and usage rights for the name and working mark.
- Product URL and social asset adaptations. The brain mark is configured as the
  WordPress Site Icon.
- Voice, terminology, and editorial style.
- Color palette, typography licenses, imagery policy, and accessibility review.
- Localization policy and supported languages.

## Interim guidance

Use plain, inclusive language. Avoid hard-coded branding in code and content fixtures; use configuration or approved design tokens when branding is finalized.
